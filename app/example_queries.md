# Recommended Stakeholder Query Library
## Google Photos AI Retrieval Discovery Engine

This library provides 22 pre-formulated natural language queries for Product Managers, UX Researchers, and Engineering Leaders. The queries map directly to the primary research questions defined in `docs/problem_statement.txt` and `docs/Architecture.md`.

---

### Category 1: Document & OCR Retrieval
1. `Why do users fail when searching for screenshots, receipts, and text in photos?`
2. `Can users retrieve prescription receipts or handwritten notes by searching words printed inside images?`
3. `What friction occurs when users try to find work documents or scanned ID cards?`

### Category 2: Facial Recognition & People Search
4. `What problems do users report with face recognition, missing face tags, and wrong person grouping?`
5. `How do users react when Google Photos fails to identify family members or mislabels faces?`
6. `Why are users unable to filter photos by multiple people or specific individuals?`

### Category 3: Timeline & Chronological Sorting
7. `How are date mismatches, wrong timestamps, and photo timeline sorting errors affecting users?`
8. `Why do downloaded or shared photos appear on the wrong dates in the main gallery view?`
9. `What issues occur when users scroll back years to find specific old vacation memories?`

### Category 4: Backup, Sync & Accidental Data Loss
10. `Why are photos disappearing or getting deleted during backup and sync operations?`
11. `What happens when users delete photos locally on their phone versus cloud backup?`
12. `How do users express panic when backup status indicators fail or show fake sync status?`

### Category 5: Privacy & Locked Folder
13. `What issues exist around locked folders, hidden photos, and passcode protection?`
14. `Why do users report locked folder items disappearing or failing to back up across devices?`

### Category 6: Storage Quotas & Cloud Sync Friction
15. `How do users experience Google One storage quotas, space full warnings, and sync blocks?`
16. `What user sentiment exists around forced cloud storage limits and subscription prompts?`

### Category 7: Natural Language & Semantic Context Search
17. `Do users complain about natural language search failing for descriptive visual memories?`
18. `Why does searching for visual objects (e.g. 'red car', 'sunset beach', 'dog') return zero results?`

### Category 8: Video & Media Processing
19. `What issues occur with video playback, missing video clips, and video sync failures?`

### Category 9: UI Navigation & Scrolling Friction
20. `What scrolling, grid navigation, and UI performance pain points do users express?`

### Category 10: Cross-Platform & Channel Patterns
21. `How do sync issues differ between iOS App Store, Android Play Store, and web users?`
22. `What are the most frequent product opportunity areas cited across Reddit and Help Community forums?`
